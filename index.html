Prep & Beyond

Privacy Policy · Last updated 16 October 2026

Prep & Beyond is a planning app for couples, from before pregnancy through to 18 months after birth. It is developed by Byarvo in Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it keeps everything on your own phone and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter, such as dates (for example a conception or birth date), checklist ticks, and appointment dates you set. This information is saved on your device, in the app's own private storage.

What we collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your information. The app has no accounts or sign-in. It has no servers of its own and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

What we share

Nothing. Because we do not collect your information, we have nothing to share with, sell to, or disclose to anyone.

Links to other websites

Some topics include a button that opens a YouTube search for that topic (for example, a how-to video). It opens in your phone's browser or the YouTube app only when you tap it. The search uses fixed topic words from the app, never anything you have entered. YouTube has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Device backups

If you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in that backup, which is stored in your own Google account and lets the data come with you if you move to a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and Google under your account's settings and privacy policy. We cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Because your information lives only on your phone, you control it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it holds.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

The app provides general information, based on Australian guidance, to help you plan and keep track. It is not medical advice and does not replace your doctor, midwife, or other health professional.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
